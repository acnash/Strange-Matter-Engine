# ISA-CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA v1 production campaign

This campaign tests an initial-state anchor in the differentiable ridge
fingerprint. The anchor contains the graph-level mean and variance of the
learned atom states immediately before recurrent evolution. These stable
chemical summaries accompany the final-state, temporal, multiscale, and
state-change-energy summaries produced by the nonlinear Graph-CA trajectory.

Screening compares trajectory-only and anchored fingerprints for the leading
credible-interval-aligned candidates. Atoms remain cells, typed bonds remain
the local neighbourhood, and every candidate retains explicit recurrent
generations, backpropagation through the transition rule, differentiable ridge
regression, scaffold-safe selection, sealed evaluation, and blind-label
exclusion. CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA is the comparison leader at point MA-ST-RAE
0.748490 and RMSE 0.847738 pIC50.

## Completed result

The campaign completed sealed evaluation on 5 September 2026. Point
MA-ST-RAE was 0.757657 and RMSE was 0.853247 pIC50. Endpoint point ST-RAE was
0.834085 for CYP1A2, 0.724457 for CYP2C9, 0.939322 for CYP2D6, and 0.532764
for CYP3A4. The initial-state anchor therefore did not improve the common
sealed holdout result. CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA remains the internal leader.
