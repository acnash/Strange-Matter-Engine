# MLR-CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA v1 production campaign

This campaign tests a multi-lag recurrence signature derived from the retained
Graph-CA trajectory. It calculates differentiable, channel-wise temporal
correlations between cellular states separated by short, intermediate, and
longer fractions of the recurrent horizon. These features expose persistence,
oscillation, and delayed recurrence that a terminal state or a single-lag
summary may omit.

The bounded screen compares the established fingerprint with and without the
multi-lag recurrence signature for every selected endpoint-rule candidate
across two scaffold folds. Successful candidates then undergo the established
five-fold, two-seed confirmation and sealed-holdout protocol.

Atoms remain chemically encoded cellular-automaton cells, typed bonds govern
local graph interactions, and nonlinear recurrent rules produce explicit
successive generations. The campaign retains trajectories, backpropagates
through every recurrent step and the genuine differentiable ridge solve, uses
scaffold-safe selection, keeps the sealed holdout isolated, and excludes blind
labels. USR-CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA remains the sealed-validation leader at point
MA-ST-RAE 0.748039 and RMSE 0.845606 pIC50.

## Completed result

The campaign completed on 12 September 2026 with sealed point MA-ST-RAE
**0.755634** and RMSE **0.846953 pIC50**. This was 0.007595 MA-ST-RAE and
0.001347 pIC50 RMSE above the USR sealed leader, so MLR remains an evaluated
experimental model and the leader is unchanged. The 1,000-resample bootstrap
mean MA-ST-RAE was 0.756380 with a 95% interval of 0.714777 to 0.798708.

The multi-lag signature was selected for all three CYP1A2 candidates,
Gray-Scott on CYP2C9, FitzHugh-Nagumo on CYP2D6, and delayed-memory dynamics on
CYP3A4. Other endpoint-rule combinations retained the established fingerprint.
The recurrence features therefore supplied useful candidate-level signal,
while the assembled sealed score remained above the leader.

The sealed holdout remained excluded from model and hyperparameter selection,
and blind labels were unavailable throughout training and evaluation.
