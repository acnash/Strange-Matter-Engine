# GDR-CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA v1 production campaign

This campaign tests whether longer recurrent cellular evolution improves
generalisation. Each leading candidate is evaluated at its established number
of generations and at 1.5 times that depth, extending the sequence of molecular
state transformations while retaining the same learned nonlinear rule.

Atoms remain chemically encoded cells and typed bonds govern local graph
interactions. The campaign preserves retained trajectories, backpropagation,
genuine differentiable ridge regression, scaffold-safe selection, sealed
holdout isolation, and blind-label exclusion. CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA remains the
comparison leader at point MA-ST-RAE 0.748490 and RMSE 0.847738 pIC50.

## Completed result

The sealed scaffold holdout produced point MA-ST-RAE **0.752414** and RMSE
**0.848947 pIC50**. This trails the leader by 0.003924 MA-ST-RAE and 0.001209
pIC50 RMSE. Longer recurrent evolution was retained as an experimental result
and was not promoted. Blind-label exclusion remained intact.
