# Strange Matter Engine challenge method report

The expanded report for the submitted **GP-CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA** system, called **GP-Graph-CA** in the manuscript, is available at its existing address:

## [Read the method, results and molecular space-time gallery](results/production_genetic_programming_graph_ca_cyp3a4_v1/GP-CIA-EA-CV-CYP-GCA_METHOD.md)

The report explains chemical encoding, atom-state trajectories, genetic programming with backpropagation, ridge regression, scaffold validation, reserved-holdout evaluation and recorded competition blind results. It ends with the ten-page gallery of fixed-rule backpropagation trajectories, with training provenance stated explicitly.

The GP submission retains the existing BP predictions for CYP1A2, CYP2C9 and CYP2D6 and uses the evolved CYP3A4 rule. Its recorded competition blind MA-ST-RAE is **0.9946**, as reported in the manuscript. These are archived submission results rather than a live leaderboard reading.
